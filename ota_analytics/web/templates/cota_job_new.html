    How a job runs

    1. Each device moves through the commands on its own, one at a time — the next 2 s after an answer.
    2. An attempt waits 30 s for the answer, then the command is sent again — up to 3 attempts, then the next command. At most about 2 minutes per command.
    3. No job runs past its time limit — 60 minutes unless you set it. A late answer is still recorded, as answered late.
    4. Devices ready for the same command go in one call, paced to the rate you set. For more than 20 devices a canary goes first, and the job pauses itself if more than 10% of commands fail.
