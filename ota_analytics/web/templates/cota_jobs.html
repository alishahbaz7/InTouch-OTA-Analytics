How a job runs

  1. Each device moves through the commands on its own, one at a time — the next at least 2 s after an answer, a 30 s guard band before a retry, up to 3 attempts.
  2. Devices ready for the same command go in one call, as many per call as the batch size allows. Calls are paced to the rate you set.
  3. A sleeping device is not sent the command again — the cloud holds it until it wakes. It is waited for, checked less and less often, until the wait runs out.
  4. It starts small: for more than 20 devices a canary goes first, and the job pauses itself if more than 10% of commands fail.
